Revine by Vials on the go

Creating your own wine Vials was never so easy. With simplicity at its core, this sleek stainless-steel state of the art machinery empowers you to create 50ml and 100ml glass tubes filled with your wine or any liquid you desire. Non-Electric, only driven by an inert gas. Vialsonthego Revined is powered by a standard inert gas tank, readily available at your local supplier. It's the perfect marriage of simplicity and quality, backed by Vialsonthego' reputation for excellence. Step into the new era of sampling innovation.
