Bottling

In our facilities you can find state of the art machinery for bottling 50 or 100 ml vials. ​Our patented technology preserves the qualities of the products by using an inert environment. The use of inert gas guarantees that the products maintain their complete bouquet, flavor, and profile throughout the fractionation process.
